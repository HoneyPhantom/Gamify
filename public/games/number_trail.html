📖 How to Play Number Trail — Rules, Tips & Strategy

Game Objective

Number Trail is an unbroken pathfinding logic puzzle rooted in topological graph theory and sequential deduction. Your objective is to link numbers in exact ascending order starting at 1 and concluding at the maximum integer N (where N equals the total number of cells in the grid, such as 25 in a 5x5 board or 36 in a 6x6 board).

Path Movement Rules & Adjacency

Every step along the trail must transition between horizontally or vertically adjacent cells (no diagonals in standard mode). Each grid square must be visited exactly once; you cannot overlap, branch, or leave isolated unvisited islands. Fixed anchor numbers are pre-placed on the board to guide your route and serve as verification checkpoints.

Topological Strategy & Dead-End Elimination

The key to mastering Number Trail is identifying "corner bottlenecks" and "perimeter traps." Corners have only two available neighbors, meaning if the trail enters a corner, it must immediately exit via the other orthogonal neighbor unless it serves as the terminal endpoint. Work backwards from the highest number as well as forward from 1, meeting in the middle to confirm unambiguous path segments.

Hint Assistance & Speed Bonuses

If you encounter an impasse, tap the single in-game hint button in the arena controls. For an escalating coin cost (10, 20, 30 coins), the engine will illuminate the next required step along the optimal Hamiltonian path. Finishing quickly awards speed bonuses and solidifies your daily sweep streak record on your profile.
