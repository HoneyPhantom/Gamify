📖 How to Play Word Guess — Rules, Tips & Strategy

Game Objective

Word Guess is a minimalist vocabulary deduction puzzle designed to test linguistic reasoning and cognitive pattern recognition. Your goal is to identify a secret five-letter mystery word within six attempts or fewer. Each guess must be a valid English word present in the official puzzle dictionary.

Color-Coded Feedback Definitions

After each submitted five-letter word, the letter tiles dynamically change color to provide deterministic feedback:

  • GREEN: The letter is in the correct position in the mystery word.
  • YELLOW: The letter is present in the mystery word, but located in a different slot.
  • GRAY: The letter does not appear anywhere in the target word.

Winning Strategies & Letter Frequency

Veteran players begin with vowel-rich starter words containing high-frequency consonants such as ARISE, CRANE, STARE, or AUDIO. By evaluating five distinct common letters on your opening turn, you immediately eliminate large sectors of the dictionary and narrow down vowel patterns (A, E, I, O, U). Avoid repeating letters confirmed to be gray, and pay close attention to common consonant pairings such as CH, SH, TH, and ending blends like CK, NG, or ER.

Escalating Hint Assistance & Coin Economy

If you find yourself stuck on the final turns, you can utilize the in-game hint button located directly on the arena controls bar. Spending 10 coins reveals an undiscovered correct letter tile on the board. Successive hints within the same puzzle session scale in cost, rewarding disciplined solvers with higher net coin yields and competitive placement on the World Wars weekly leaderboards.
