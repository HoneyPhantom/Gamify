📖 How to Play Mini Crossword — Rules, Clues & Solving Strategy

Game Objective

Mini Crossword is an engaging daily word game that challenges both your vocabulary and general trivia knowledge. Set within a compact 5x5 grid, your objective is to fill every white cell with a single letter such that the letters spell out valid intersecting words corresponding to Across and Down clue descriptions.

Grid Navigation & Direction Controls

Tapping or clicking on any white letter cell activates it and highlights the current word track. Tapping the cell a second time toggles the typing direction between horizontal (Across) and vertical (Down). You can easily navigate across cells using on-screen keyboard buttons, standard physical keyboard arrows, or the Tab key to jump straight to the next sequential clue number.

Interlocking Clue Deduction

The fundamental strategy in crossword puzzles relies on crossing intersections. If an Across clue has several potential answers, check the intersecting Down clues. Letters you establish from easier Down clues immediately reveal confirmed anchor letters in your uncertain Across words, rapidly eliminating ambiguous options. Always scan through all Across and Down clues at the beginning of a session to solve the most obvious answers first.

Escalating Hint Assistance & Coin Economy

When faced with obscure trivia or an unfamiliar synonym, players can tap the single unified Hint button on the header control panel. Using a hint reveals the correct letter for the currently focused grid square. The first hint costs 10 coins, with subsequent hints scaling gradually in cost within the same puzzle session. Solvers who finish the puzzle independently with minimal hints receive maximum leaderboard bonus points and tournament championship standings.
