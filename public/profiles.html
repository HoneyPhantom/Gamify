📖 About Gamify Player Profiles & Progression System ▼ Expand

Welcome to the official player profile hub on Gamify. Every registered puzzle enthusiast is assigned a unique gamertag, cryptographic identifier, and persistent skill ledger that tracks puzzle-solving prowess across six unique daily brain challenges: Word Guess, Number Grid, Classic Sudoku, Memory Match, Number Trail, and the Daily Crossword.

Experience Points (XP) & Player Levels: Whenever you conquer a daily puzzle, your profile is credited with experience points calibrated by puzzle complexity and speed bonuses. As your XP grows, your rank advances from Novice to Apprentice, Strategist, Master, and ultimately Grandmaster. Your avatar frame visually showcases your earned prestige to fellow contenders across the global arena.

Consecutive Daily Streaks: Consistency is the cornerstone of cognitive agility. Gamify tracks consecutive calendar day completions calculated precisely at UTC midnight. Solving all daily sweep games awards bonus coins and elevates your historic best streak badge. If a day is missed, active streaks reset while your historic high watermark remains permanently celebrated on your profile.

Social Privacy Controls: Gamify gives players complete sovereignty over their social footprint. Accounts marked as Public allow community members to inspect follower networks and challenge players directly. Setting your account to Private conceals your follower roster from non-followers while preserving your standing on the World Wars global leaderboards.
