📖 About the Gamify Global Leaderboard & Scoring Mechanics

Global Ranking Architecture

The Gamify Global Leaderboard represents the definitive worldwide ranking of cognitive puzzle solvers. Players compete daily across diverse disciplines including vocabulary deduction, spatial arithmetic, pattern memorization, and classical Sudoku logic. Rankings are computed deterministically using running aggregate tables backed by Cloudflare D1 SQL, ensuring instant response times and tamper-proof verification.

Score Computation & Time Decay Formulas

Every puzzle completion awards points based on difficulty tier (Easy: 100 pts, Medium: 250 pts, Hard: 500 pts). In addition, speed bonuses reward rapid solvers who complete puzzles without excessive pauses. For time-sensitive challenges, score modifiers reward efficient moves and penalize unnecessary errors. Players also receive consecutive streak multipliers, where maintaining an unbroken daily streak provides an escalating percentage boost to total leaderboard score.

Fair Play & Dynamic Profile Integration

Every entrant on the leaderboard is a registered Gamify player with a verifiable dynamic profile template (accessible via profiles?id=...). Solvers can inspect each contender's earned cosmetic titles, custom avatar frames, follower networks, and game-by-game statistics. To maintain integrity, all puzzle solutions are validated server-side by our Cloudflare Worker APIs before scores are committed to the global leaderboard ledger.
