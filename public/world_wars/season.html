📖 How Tournament Results & Top 10 Verification Work

Championship Contender Qualification

The Gamify World Wars weekly tournament leaderboard features the top 10 ranked contenders who have accumulated the highest tournament points over a seven-day cycle. Qualification is completely merit-based: points are awarded upon successful completion of daily logic puzzles, with difficulty multipliers determining the total points earned per puzzle.

Tie-Breaking & Query Optimization

When two or more contenders finish a season with identical point totals, rankings are deterministically resolved by total games played and puzzle completion speed. The underlying database queries are executed over pre-indexed Cloudflare D1 tables (weekly_tournament), guaranteeing millisecond response times and negligible server resource overhead even during peak global traffic hours.

Prize Distribution & Cosmetic Badges

The champion crowned at Rank #1 at Sunday midnight receives an exclusive seasonal gold profile badge, bonus cosmetic shop coins, and permanent commemoration in our historical archives. Second and third place finishers are awarded silver and bronze championship badges. All registered contenders retain their verified seasonal statistics on their personal dynamic profile pages.
