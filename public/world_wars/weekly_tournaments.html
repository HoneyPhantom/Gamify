📖 How World Wars Weekly Tournaments Work — Rules, Scoring & Rewards

Weekly Tournament Format & Reset Schedules

The World Wars championship is a weekly synchronous tournament designed to test consistent daily cognitive agility. Each season runs from Monday 00:00 UTC through Sunday 23:59 UTC. When the clock strikes midnight on Sunday, final standings are permanently locked into the season archive, and an all-new tournament season commences automatically.

Championship Points & Progression System

Contenders accrue tournament points by successfully completing daily puzzle challenges. Difficult puzzles (such as Hard Sudoku or Expert Number Grid) yield maximum points, while speed bonuses reward rapid deduction. Solvers can also accumulate bonus points by initiating PvP friend challenges. Maintaining active participation across all seven days ensures maximum point accrual for the weekly leaderboard.

Dedicated Dynamic Season Pages & Archival Queries

Every tournament season (e.g., /world_wars/weekly_tournaments/S2026-W44) possesses its own dedicated dynamic template page. This page queries Cloudflare D1 with high-efficiency compound indices to instantly render the top 10 prize contenders alongside the logged-in solver's personal rank, total points, and games played. Past season records remain permanently accessible, forming an indelible historical record of puzzle mastery.
