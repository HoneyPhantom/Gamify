📖 About the Gamify Cosmetic Economy & Fair Play Policy

Strictly Cosmetic, Non-Pay-To-Win Philosophy

Gamify operates on an uncompromising free-to-play fair-play standard. All items showcased in the cosmetic shop — including neon avatar frames, animated borders, and prestigious profile titles — are 100% aesthetic. No item grants in-game puzzle solving advantages, time extensions, or automated solutions. Every solver stands on equal analytical footing.

Earning In-Game Currency

Coins cannot be purchased with real money. Solvers accumulate coins exclusively through cognitive achievement:

  • Solving daily puzzle sweeps (Easy: 10 coins, Medium: 25 coins, Hard: 50 coins).
  • Maintaining consecutive daily play streaks, unlocking milestone coin chests.
  • Finishing in the top tier of weekly World Wars tournament championships.

Equipping & Dynamic Profile Rendering

Once unlocked, cosmetics are permanently bound to the user's account in our Cloudflare D1 database. Solvers can equip their favorite frame and honor title from their profile management drawer, rendering immediately on dynamic public profile pages (profiles?id=...) and leaderboard standings.
