📖 How Asynchronous PvP Challenges Work — Rules & Matchmaking

Asynchronous Fair Play Engine

Gamify PvP Challenges allow puzzle solvers to compete 1-on-1 without requiring concurrent live connections. When Player A initiates a duel, the server generates a fixed pseudorandom seed. Player A solves the generated puzzle, recording their time taken, moves, and final score. An invitation is then dispatched to Player B.

Identical Puzzle Board Guarantees

When Player B accepts, they are presented with the exact same initial letter arrangement, number grid distribution, or Sudoku seed. Because both competitors encounter identical difficulty parameters, victory is determined purely by solving speed and analytical accuracy. The solver with the higher score or faster clear time automatically claims the match victory.

Coin Stakes & Escrow Security

Challenges can be played for friendly glory or with optional coin wagers. When a wager is specified, both players' coin stakes are held in server escrow. Upon match completion, the victor receives the combined pot alongside World Wars bonus points. Duels that remain unanswered for over 48 hours expire automatically, refunding all held coins to the challenger.
