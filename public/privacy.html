Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026 · Official Gamify Publisher Policy

Welcome to Gamify (accessible at gamify.pages.dev). We are dedicated to delivering free, high-quality daily puzzle games while rigorously respecting the privacy and data rights of our players worldwide. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we comply with industry privacy regulations and Google Publisher Policies.

1. Information We Collect and Store

Gamify operates with a privacy-first, minimal-data philosophy. You can play all puzzles as a guest without creating an account or providing sensitive personal information.

  • Account Data: If you choose to register an account or link a profile, we store a public username, an optional display name, an optional bio, and a securely salted and hashed 4-digit PIN (using standard Web Crypto SHA-256 algorithms). We never store plaintext passwords or security PINs.
  • Gameplay & Puzzle Statistics: To track your consecutive daily streaks, solve times, coin balances, unlocks, and leaderboard rankings, our servers store puzzle completion scores and timestamps.
  • Local Storage and IndexedDB: Gamify utilizes your web browser's local storage and IndexedDB technologies to cache puzzle boards, cosmetic unlocks, and active streaks locally on your device for fast 0ms offline startup.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

In accordance with Google Publisher Policies and AdSense compliance standards, we disclose the following concerning third-party advertising partners:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to Gamify and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Log Files, Analytics, and Cloudflare Edge Network

Like most web applications, Gamify and our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and Pages) automatically log basic technical requests when your browser interacts with our servers. These server logs may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser user agent, internet service provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These logs are used strictly for technical performance monitoring, DDoS mitigation, bot prevention, and system maintenance.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Gamify uses essential cookies and local session tokens solely to keep registered players authenticated across browser visits. Advertising partners such as Google AdSense may deploy their own cookies, web beacons, and JavaScript tags to measure advertising effectiveness and deliver relevant sponsored content. We do not have direct access to or control over cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA & CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to specific rights regarding their personal data, including the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of any personal data collected by Gamify.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (Gamify does not sell personal user data to any data brokers).
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising their privacy rights.

6. GDPR Privacy Rights (European Economic Area Residents)

If you are an EEA or UK resident, you hold data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and request portability of your stored player data. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of young children is paramount. Gamify does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out directly:

Email: privacy@gamify.tech or our Contact Page
Website: gamify.pages.dev
