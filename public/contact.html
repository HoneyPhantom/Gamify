Frequently Asked Questions

When do the daily puzzles reset?

All daily puzzle games reset simultaneously at 00:00 UTC every single day. Streak counters and Daily Sweep challenges evaluate against this calendar milestone.

How does the daily streak calculation work?

Your streak count increases when you successfully solve a daily puzzle on consecutive UTC calendar days. If a day is missed, the active streak resets to 0 while your all-time best streak is permanently recorded on your profile.

Can I play Gamify offline on my mobile phone?

Yes! Gamify is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Simply tap your browser menu and select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen" to install Gamify directly on your home screen with 0ms offline startup.
